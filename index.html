When Should You Refinance Your Mortgage?

Refinancing a mortgage replaces your existing home loan with a new loan featuring updated interest rates, term lengths, or payment schedules. The fundamental rule of refinancing is ensuring you plan to stay in the property longer than the break-even period required to recoup closing costs.

How the Break-Even Point is Calculated:

Break-Even Period (Months) = Total Refinance Closing Costs / Monthly Payment Savings

If closing costs are $6,000 and your monthly principal & interest payment drops by $200, your break-even horizon is 30 months (2.5 years). If you sell or move before 30 months, refinancing will result in a net financial loss.
